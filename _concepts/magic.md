# {{ page.title}}

Magic is the power by which we manipulate the world. Magic takes many forms, but a few are detailed below.

## Casting

Mages can use magic to directly influence the world by casting spells. This is exceedingly difficult to do, so mages often offload this task to some form of foci, such as an orb.

When using a focus, the mage provides the mana and direction, but the focus holds and casts the spell. While greatly easing the difficulty of casting, this has it's drawbacks, including limiting variety of spells that can be cast.

## Artificing

Artificing consists of making permanent alterations to the physical world by use of magic. Foci and other magical equipment are the most common products of artifice.
